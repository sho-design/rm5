Clinical infusion

Iron therapy, when your body needs more than supplements.

This is a clinical infusion, not a wellness drip. It is lab-reviewed, physician-directed, and given with monitoring, for when low iron is holding you back and tablets have not been enough.

Book an assessmentTalk to us about iron
Who it is for
Low ferritin or diagnosed iron deficiencyIron-deficiency anemiaFatigue related to low ironHeavy menstrual bleedingIron supplements that have not worked or are not tolerated
{{ s.n }}{{ s.t }}{{ s.d }}
Safety and monitoringModern intravenous iron is well tolerated, and we treat it with the seriousness it deserves. You are screened first and monitored throughout. We will not proceed if it is not the right or safe choice for you.
On coverage, honestlyUnlike elective wellness services, iron therapy may be clinically indicated. Some private plans may help with cost, and we will guide you through what to ask. We cannot promise coverage.
Already a Restoration patient?If your bloodwork flags low iron, you can be referred in-house. Ask your physician, or mention it when you book, and we will arrange the pathway.
Let us look at your iron, properly.Bring recent bloodwork if you have it. We will assess, explain your options clearly, and only proceed when it is the right call.
