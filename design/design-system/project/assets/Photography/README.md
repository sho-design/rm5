# Photography

Nine photos from the website: the five division heroes, reception, an iron infusion visit, an aesthetics consult, a doctor with a family, and the portrait of Dr. Johnny Nguyen. These are generated images, so present them as illustrations of the clinic, never as real patients.

- Editorial daylight, white and soft grey interiors, black or white chairs and tables only. No text in images.
- About 45% of people shown are Vietnamese or East Asian.
- Nurses wear black V-neck scrubs, doctors navy V-neck scrubs, in a modern tailored fit; everyone on staff wears white sneakers. Dr. Johnny Nguyen usually wears a white tailored lab coat over navy scrubs and white canvas sneakers with a red stripe on the sole. No visible brand logos.
- Doctors wear light blue nitrile gloves; aesthetic nurses wear black. No stethoscope on nurses unless it is relevant.
- Ultrasound is a wireless handheld scanner with the image on a phone or tablet. Never a cart machine or a probe cable.
- On the site photos sit in `radius-block` (28px) frames, often under the `--photo-protection` gradient with white .92 pill captions. Anchor each crop on the top of the main head so no head is cut off.
