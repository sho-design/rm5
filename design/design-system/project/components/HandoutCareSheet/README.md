# HandoutCareSheet

One-page Letter care sheet for steps a patient follows around a treatment or a medicine: tinted hero, two numbered cards (before and after, or one per medicine), what to expect, one sunshine panel for what must not be missed, a navy "Call us if" band and the document footer.

**You provide:** the treatment name and a one-paragraph description, the before and after steps, what is normal, the must-not-miss rules, the call-us signs with a time, and the document code, version and approval record.

- For a medication sheet, swap the two cards for one card per medicine: an eyebrow ("Before treatment", "After treatment"), the medicine name, a one-line rhythm ("Nightly. Build up slowly."), schedule tiles or numbered application rows, bullets, and a closing callout. The dose given in clinic is the first row, tinted sage.
- The tint, header pill, eyebrow and bullet dots follow the service line, as on the website: lilac (`div-aesthetics`, `rm-lilac-text`) for this Medical Aesthetics sheet. The sunshine panel uses amber dots.
- Static HTML preview at 816 by 1056px (Letter at 96dpi), recreated from the clinic's PicoSure Pro sheet in the Medical Aesthetics lilac, with middots replaced by commas, the RM monogram tile with the name in Fraunces in the header, and the IV therapy line removed because it reads as a benefit claim.
