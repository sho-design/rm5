Pre and post care

PicoSure Pro. Your skin, before and after.

PicoSure Pro is a picosecond laser that delivers very short pulses of energy to the skin. Depending on the lens and settings, it is used for unwanted pigmentation, sun damage, melasma, uneven skin tone and acne scars. A series of 4 to 8 sessions is recommended.

1Before your treatment

Your provider sets your pre-treatment plan. It may include:

  • Hydroquinone cream. A thin layer on the targeted areas as directed, then your moisturizer.
  • Tranexamic acid. Take as prescribed by your provider.
  • Medical-grade skincare. Vitamin C and pigment products chosen for your skin.

2After your treatment

  • Cleanse with a gentle, fragrance-free, alcohol-free cleanser.
  • Apply the recommended skincare to keep skin hydrated.
  • Avoid sun for 2 to 4 weeks, or until the skin has fully healed.
  • Broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, with UVA and UVB protection.
  • Avoid heat, including hot yoga and saunas.
  • Resume exfoliation only once the skin is completely healed.

What to expect

  • Pigmented areas may look darker at first before they gradually fade.
  • Mild redness, swelling or sensitivity.
  • Skin may feel warm or rough, like fine sandpaper, for a short time.
  • Small crusts or dark spots that shed as the skin heals.

Do not pick, scratch, peel or remove any crusting or flaking.

Sun and heat protection Very important

Essential after PicoSure Pro, especially when treating pigmentation or melasma.

  • SPF 30 to 50+ every day; reapply in the sun.
  • Hat and shade whenever possible.
  • No tanning or tanning beds.
  • Limit direct sun for 2 to 4 weeks, or as directed.
  • First 5 days: no saunas, steam rooms, hot tubs or very hot showers.
  • Do not keep a mask over the treated area for long.
