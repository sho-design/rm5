Vaccines

The HPV vaccine.

HPV is a common virus spread through intimate skin contact. Some types can cause cervical, throat and other cancers. The vaccine protects against the types that cause most of them, and works best before any exposure.

What it is

A short series of shots in the upper arm. In Ontario it is free for students in Grade 7, and missed doses can be caught up during high school. Adults up to 26 are recommended to get it. Older adults can still benefit; ask us if it fits you.

When and how often

  • Grade 7, through the school program
  • Missed it? Catch up any time up to age 26
  • Over 26: talk with us about your situation
  • Vaccinated? You still need regular cervical screening

What to expect

1A quick nurse screen and questions about allergies
2One shot in the upper arm. A sore arm for a day is common
3Your record is updated and we tell you if another dose is due

Book a visit sooner if

  • You have a weakened immune system; you may need 3 doses
  • You are pregnant; the vaccine is usually given after pregnancy
  • You are unsure what you received as a child
