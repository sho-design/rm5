# HandoutTopicSheet

One-page Letter topic sheet that explains one test, vaccine or condition and when to book: a tinted hero with three fact tiles beside a photo, "What it is" and "When and how often", a navy "What to expect" band with three steps, a sunshine "Book a visit sooner if" panel, the contact card with a QR code, and the clinic footer with hours.

**You provide:** the topic title (ending in a full stop), a short plain-language paragraph, three facts you can stand behind (a figure and a short label), the photo, the when-and-how-often bullets, the three visit steps, the book-sooner signs, the QR target page, and the document code and version.

- The tint, header pill, bullet dots and eyebrow follow the service line, as on the website: sky (`div-family`, `rm-sky-text`) for every Family Medicine sheet, including vaccines and cancer screening.
- Use a photo that follows the Photography rules; crop so heads are not cut off. The QR code must be generated for the real page and scanned before printing; the preview shows its slot only.
- Static HTML preview at 816 by 1056px, recreated from the clinic's HPV vaccine sheet (RM-FAM-PTE-101) with the RM monogram tile and the name in Fraunces in the header. See the Patient handouts section for every rule.
